About the USANT Library

A central learning hub for the Anthonian community

The USANT Library is a central learning hub that supports academic excellence through print and digital collections, research support, and welcoming study spaces. It helps the university community access knowledge, information, and services both on campus and online.

  • 1

    Provide library materials and services responsive to the needs of the USANT community.

  • 2

    Ensure maximum accessibility to library resources.

  • 3

    Maintain comfortable, convenient, safe, accessible, and well-maintained facilities.

  • 4

    Develop effective and committed library personnel.

  • 5

    Improve access to collections and services through automated library systems.

USANT Library Rules, Art. 09

  • Students should be in proper uniform with I.D. when entering the library.
  • Silence shall be maintained in the library. Failure to observe this rule shall be subject to serious disciplinary action.
  • Stealing or willfully destroying, cutting, tearing, or in any way damaging books, pamphlets, periodicals, or any part thereof shall be subject to serious disciplinary action.

  • Students should be in proper uniform with I.D. when entering the library.
  • Students shall present their library card for Grade School, Junior High School, and Senior High Students with a picture.

  • General references such as dictionaries, encyclopedias, almanacs, atlases, and instructional materials shall be read only inside the library.
  • General reference collection, theses, dissertations, and periodicals are to be used inside the library only.
  • Faculty members shall be the ones to borrow reference books for their use.

  • Thesis and dissertations should be read inside the library.
  • These materials form part of the Graduate School Library’s major research collection and are non-circulating.

The official USANT Library publication does not list a separate device policy; the general rules on silence, proper conduct, and care of library materials apply to the use of laptops and electronic devices inside the library.

Not separately published For current device guidelines, please inquire at the library office or contact the library.

  • Faculty and staff can borrow two (2) books — one (1) Filipiniana and one (1) circulation — for a period of one (1) week only; students can borrow two (2) books — one (1) Filipiniana and one (1) circulation — for three (3) days.
  • A week before the final examination, borrowing of books is suspended / cancelled.
  • Circulation books may be borrowed and subject to renewal depending upon the number of copies available at the time they are borrowed.
  • Two (2) books can be loaned out for a day from the circulation, excluding holidays and Sunday. Filipiniana collection can be loaned for a day, one (1) at a time, excluding holidays and Sunday.
  • Reserve books for overnight use may be borrowed at 4:00 P.M. and returned at 9:00 A.M. the following day. Failure to return them on time will result in a fine of Php 1.00 per hour.
  • Fiction / circulation books may be borrowed for a period of three (3) days. Failure to return them on time will result in a fine of Php 9.00 per day.
  • Borrowers are allowed to photocopy the selected topic or chapter of the borrowed book.
  • Lost books must be replaced with the same author, title, and edition, binding and copyright date; if not possible, full payment of the same plus fine from the time the book is lost shall be made at the cashier’s office.

  • The borrower shall be responsible for the loaned book. In case of loss or damage, they shall be required either to pay or replace it with the same title upon arrangement with the librarian.
  • Stealing or willfully destroying, cutting, tearing, or in any way damaging the books, pamphlets, periodicals, or any part thereof shall be subject to serious disciplinary action.

The official USANT Library publication does not currently publish a separate outside-researchers policy. Researchers from other institutions are encouraged to coordinate directly with the library office for access arrangements.

Mission & Objectives

The University library is an integral part of the educational program of the University. Its basic objective is to aid the institution in carrying out its program in order to provide quality education.

The measure of its excellence is the extent to which its resources and services support the institution’s objective by acquiring and making available books, periodicals, and services needed in the institutional program.

Adopted Objectives

  1. To select, acquire, organize and provide library materials that will enrich and support the curriculum and personal needs of the users/clientele, taking into consideration their varied interests, abilities, and learning styles.
  2. To provide library materials that will stimulate growth in factual knowledge, literary appreciation, aesthetic values, and ethical standards.
  3. To teach formally the use of books and other sources of information.
  4. To provide background information which will enable students to make intelligent judgments on their daily lives.
  5. To coordinate with the faculty and supply their needs for instructional materials.
  6. To place principle above personal opinion and reason above prejudice in the selection of materials of the highest quality in order to assure a comprehensive library collection appropriate for users/customers.
  7. To supply essential information to the library’s clientele.
